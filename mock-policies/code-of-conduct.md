# Code of Conduct

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct outlines the standards of professional behavior expected from all employees and contractors at our organization.

## Core Values

- **Integrity:** We conduct ourselves with honesty and transparency
- **Respect:** We treat all individuals with dignity and respect
- **Accountability:** We take responsibility for our actions
- **Collaboration:** We work together toward common goals
- **Excellence:** We strive for quality in everything we do

## Professional Conduct

All employees must:
- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism
- Refrain from harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations

## Workplace Safety

- Maintain a safe working environment
- Report any safety concerns immediately to your manager
- Participate in required safety training
- Never engage in behavior that puts yourself or others at risk

## Violation and Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension
- Termination of employment

All investigations will be handled confidentially and fairly.

## Questions

For questions regarding this policy, please contact the HR Department at hr@company.com
