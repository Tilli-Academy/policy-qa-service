# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

**Version:** 1.3  
**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

## Eligible Expenses

The following business expenses may be reimbursed:
- Transportation (flights, trains, taxis, mileage)
- Accommodation during business travel
- Meals during business travel (reasonable amounts)
- Conference and training registration fees
- Business-related supplies and materials
- Client entertainment (with manager approval)

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal items (toiletries, entertainment)
- Alcoholic beverages (except client entertainment approved)
- Traffic violations or parking tickets
- Personal travel insurance
- Laundry and dry cleaning
- Items for personal use

## Submission Requirements

- All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence
- Original receipts must be provided for amounts over $25
- Business purpose and attendees must be documented
- Expenses should be categorized correctly in the expense system

## Approval Process

- Up to $500: Manager approval
- $500 - $2,500: Department head approval
- Over $2,500: CFO approval
- All international expenses require director-level approval

## Mileage Reimbursement

- Current rate: $0.67 per mile
- Only business miles are reimbursable
- Personal commute is not eligible
- Use GPS or mapping app to document distance

## Travel Guidelines

- Book travel 2 weeks in advance when possible
- Use pre-approved travel vendors to get best rates
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Business class requires VP approval
- Hotel should be near business location

## Submission and Questions

Submit expenses through the expense management portal at expenses.company.com or contact finance at finance@company.com
