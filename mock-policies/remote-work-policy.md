# Remote Work Policy

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025

## Overview

This policy governs remote work arrangements for eligible employees and ensures business continuity while supporting work-life balance.

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible for remote work if:
- They have been with the company for at least 6 months
- Their role allows for remote execution
- They have manager approval
- They meet performance standards

The following roles are NOT eligible:
- Reception and security personnel
- Manufacturing and on-site support roles
- Roles requiring specialized equipment

## Remote Work Schedules

Three models are available:
- **Fully Remote:** 5 days per week from home or co-working space
- **Hybrid:** 3 days remote, 2 days in office (specific days by team agreement)
- **Flexible:** Arrangement with manager, at least 1 day in office per week

## Work Environment Requirements

Remote workers must:
- Maintain a dedicated, quiet workspace
- Ensure adequate lighting and ergonomic setup
- Maintain reliable high-speed internet connection
- Secure network with WPA3 or equivalent encryption
- Use company VPN for all work-related activities

## Equipment and Allowance

- Company provides laptop and monitor
- Additional equipment requests must be approved by manager
- $300 annual home office allowance for approved equipment
- Internet and utilities are employee responsibility

## Communication and Collaboration

- Attend all scheduled team meetings (in-person or virtual)
- Maintain regular communication with team members
- Be available during core hours (9 AM - 4 PM local time)
- Respond to messages within 2 hours during business hours

## Office Days and Expectations

- Attendance on office days is mandatory (except for approved time off)
- Office days are used for collaboration, training, and team building
- Lunch arrangements must be notified to team leads
- Use of hot-desking is acceptable on non-assigned office days

## Performance and Monitoring

- Remote workers are evaluated on output and results, not hours
- Managers will conduct regular check-ins
- No surveillance tools or keystroke monitoring allowed
- Productivity is monitored through project management systems

## Termination of Remote Work

Remote work privileges may be terminated if:
- Performance standards are not met
- Regular communication is not maintained
- Company needs require return to office
- Employee requests termination

Notice period: 30 days

## Expenses and Reimbursement

Remote workers are responsible for:
- Internet and utility costs
- Home office supplies (subject to annual allowance)
- Commuting costs on office days

These expenses are not separately reimbursable.

## Questions

For questions about remote work, contact HR at hr@company.com
