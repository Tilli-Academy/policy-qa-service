# Information Security Policy

**Version:** 2.1  
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2024

## Overview

This policy establishes security requirements for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors to protect company information assets.

## Access Control

- All employees must use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems
- Access rights must be reviewed quarterly
- Inactive accounts will be disabled after 30 days

## Data Classification

Data is classified into four levels:
- **Public:** Can be freely shared externally
- **Internal:** Restricted to employees only
- **Confidential:** Limited distribution within approved departments
- **Restricted:** Maximum protection, accessed on need-to-know basis only

## Device Security

- All company-provided devices must have encryption enabled
- Antivirus software must be kept up to date
- Screen locks are required when away from desk
- Personal devices must comply with BYOD policy if accessing company data

## Incident Response

- Security incidents must be reported within 1 hour of discovery
- Report to: security-team@company.com
- Do not attempt to investigate or remediate without authorization
- All incidents will be documented and reviewed

## Remote Work Security

- Use company VPN for all remote work
- Secure your home network with strong encryption
- Do not work in public spaces without privacy screens
- Use noise-canceling headphones to prevent eavesdropping

## Non-Compliance

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
