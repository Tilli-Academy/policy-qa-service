# Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.2  
**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

## Introduction

We are committed to protecting the privacy of personal data. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect personal information.

## Data We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data:
- Contact information (name, email, phone number)
- Employment information (job title, department, employment history)
- Financial information (bank details for payroll)
- Health information (for benefits administration)
- Biometric data (for access control, where applicable)

## Use of Data

Personal data is used for:
- Employment and HR administration
- Payroll processing
- Benefits management
- Communication and notifications
- Security and access control
- Compliance with legal obligations

## Data Protection

- All personal data is stored securely with encryption
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
- Data is retained only as long as necessary
- Regular backups are maintained with secure recovery procedures

## Employee Rights

Employees have the right to:
- Access their personal data
- Request corrections to inaccurate data
- Understand how their data is used
- Request deletion of data (subject to legal requirements)
- File a complaint with relevant data protection authorities

## Third-Party Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties except:
- With written consent
- As required by law
- For business operations (payroll processors, insurance providers)

## Data Breach Notification

Any suspected data breach must be reported immediately to the security team. Affected individuals will be notified within 72 hours of discovery.

## Policy Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. Significant changes will be communicated to all employees.
