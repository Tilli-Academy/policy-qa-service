# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Version:** 1.5  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

## Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

### Accrual
- Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month
- Unused leave does not carry over to the next year
- Maximum of 5 days can be carried forward with manager approval

### Application Process
- Submit leave requests at least 2 weeks in advance
- Requests must be submitted through the HR portal
- Manager approval is required
- Emergency leave should be communicated as soon as possible

## Sick Leave

- 10 days of paid sick leave per year
- Medical certification may be required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
- Unused sick leave does not carry over

## Public Holidays

Company observes the following public holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Independence Day
- Thanksgiving
- Christmas

Employees working on public holidays receive time off in lieu (TOIL).

## Unpaid Leave

- Extended unpaid leave requires 30 days notice
- Maximum 30 days per year with manager approval
- No benefits accrual during unpaid leave period

## Parental Leave

- Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid
- Adoption leave: 4 weeks paid

## Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 days paid bereavement leave for immediate family members.

## Approval and Questions

All leave matters should be directed to HR at hr@company.com or your direct manager.
