## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines eligibility, limits, and expectations for working remotely (working from home or another approved location). It applies to all permanent employees. It does not change contractual working hours or leave entitlements, which are governed by the Leave Policy (POL-HR-001).

## 2. Definitions

- **Remote work / work from home (WFH):** performing contracted duties from an approved location other than a company office.
- **Hybrid working:** a mix of office and remote days within the same week.
- **Standard working hours:** 09:00–17:30, Monday–Friday, with a 60-minute unpaid lunch (37.5 hours per week).
- **Core hours:** 10:00–16:00 local time, during which all employees must be available regardless of location.

## 3. Remote Work Eligibility

- Employees become eligible for regular remote work after **successfully completing the 6-month probation period**.
- The employee's role must be classified as **remote-suitable** by the department head. Roles that require on-site presence (for example, facilities, reception, and hardware lab roles) are classified as **on-site** and are not eligible for regular remote work.
- Employees must have a **"meets expectations"** or higher rating in their most recent performance review.
- Employees in their probation period may work remotely on an **occasional, pre-approved basis** (for example, for a deliverance appointment) but are expected to work primarily on-site.

## 4. Work-From-Home Limits

- Eligible employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. At least **2 days per week must be worked on-site**.
- Fully remote arrangements (0 on-site days) are **exceptions** and require approval from both the **department head** and the **People Team**, and must be documented in PeopleHub.
- Teams may designate up to **one fixed "anchor day" per week** on which all team members work on-site for collaboration. Anchor days are published in PeopleHub at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Working from a location in a **different country** requires prior approval from the **People Team** (for tax and legal reasons) and is limited to **20 working days per leave year**.

## 5. Working Hours

- Remote working hours are the **same** as standard working hours (Section 2). Remote work does not change contracted hours.
- Employees must be **online and reachable during core hours (10:00–16:00)**.
- Any change to an employee's daily schedule (for example, a shifted start time) must be agreed in writing with the line manager and must preserve availability during core hours.
- Overtime rules are unchanged by location and follow the employee's contract.

## 6. Attendance Expectations

- Employees must record their work location (office or remote) in **PeopleHub** each working day, or set a recurring weekly pattern.
- Employees working remotely must:
  - be reachable via the company collaboration tools during core hours;
  - respond to messages within a reasonable time (normally **within 2 hours** during working hours);
  - attend all scheduled meetings by video where requested.
- Repeated unavailability during core hours without an agreed exception may result in **suspension of the remote work arrangement** by the line manager, following a documented discussion.
- Remote work is **not a substitute for leave or dependant care**. Employees needing time off must request leave under the Leave Policy (POL-HR-001).

## 7. Remote Work Approval Process

1. The employee submits a **Remote Work Request** in **PeopleHub**, specifying the proposed pattern (for example, "Tuesday and Thursday remote").
2. The request routes to the **line manager**, who confirms the role is remote-suitable and that team coverage is maintained.
3. The line manager **approves or declines within 5 working days**, recording a reason for any decline (for example, insufficient on-site coverage).
4. **Fully remote** or **different-country** requests additionally route to the **People Team** for approval after the line manager.
5. Approved arrangements are recorded in PeopleHub and are **reviewed at each performance review** (at least annually). A manager may revise an arrangement with **4 weeks' notice** and a documented business reason.

## 8. Equipment and Security

- The company provides a laptop and required software. Employees must follow the **IT Security Policy (POL-IT-001)** at all times when working remotely, including device encryption, MFA, and secure handling of company data.
- Company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
