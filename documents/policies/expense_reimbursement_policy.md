## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines which business expenses the company reimburses, the limits that apply, and how to claim. It applies to all employees. All amounts are in **US dollars (USD)**. Expenses are claimed through the **SpendDesk** system.

## 2. General Principles

- Expenses must be **reasonable, necessary, and incurred wholly for business purposes**.
- Employees should choose the most cost-effective option that still meets the business need.
- Employees **may not approve their own expenses** (see Section 8).
- Attempting to claim personal or inflated expenses is treated as a disciplinary matter.

## 3. Eligible Expenses

The following are reimbursable within the limits in Section 5:

- **Business travel:** standard-class rail, economy-class flights, and essential taxis or ride-share for business trips.
- **Accommodation:** hotel stays required for business travel.
- **Meals:** reasonable meals while travelling on business (subject to the daily meal allowance).
- **Mileage:** use of a personal vehicle for business travel, reimbursed at **$0.55 per mile**.
- **Client entertainment:** meals or events with clients, with prior line-manager approval and a recorded business purpose and attendee list.
- **Professional development:** pre-approved training, courses, and conference fees.
- **Work-from-home equipment:** a one-time ergonomic allowance of up to **$250** for eligible remote workers (see Remote Work Policy, POL-HR-002), claimed once per employment.
- **Professional memberships:** one role-relevant membership per year, with line-manager approval.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **never reimbursed**:

- Personal travel, personal entertainment, or personal purchases.
- Commuting between home and the employee's usual office.
- Traffic or parking fines, speeding tickets, and other penalties.
- Alcohol, except modest amounts as part of an approved **client entertainment** event.
- First-class or business-class travel (unless pre-approved in writing for flights over **8 hours**).
- In-room hotel entertainment, minibar, and spa or leisure charges.
- Expenses already paid for or reimbursed by a third party.
- Any expense claimed without a valid receipt where a receipt is required (Section 6).

## 5. Spending Limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Meals (domestic travel) | **$60 per day** | Covers all meals for the day |
| Meals (international travel) | **$90 per day** | Covers all meals for the day |
| Hotel (domestic) | **$200 per night** | Excludes taxes; above limit needs pre-approval |
| Hotel (international) | **$300 per night** | Excludes taxes; above limit needs pre-approval |
| Taxi / ride-share | **$75 per trip** | Use public transport where practical |
| Client entertainment | **$100 per attendee** | Requires prior line-manager approval |
| Flights over 8 hours | Business class permitted | Requires written pre-approval |
| Work-from-home equipment | **$250 one-time** | Once per employment |

- Any **single expense over $1,000** requires **pre-approval** from the line manager **before** it is incurred.
- Any **single expense over $5,000** requires pre-approval from the **department head and the Finance Team** before it is incurred.
- Limits are **maximums**, not entitlements; claim only what was actually spent.

## 6. Receipt Requirements

- An **itemized receipt** is required for **every expense of $25 or more**.
- For expenses **under $25**, a receipt is preferred; where none is available, the employee may submit a brief written explanation in SpendDesk.
- Receipts must clearly show the **date, vendor, amount, and items purchased**. Credit-card statements alone are **not** acceptable as itemized receipts.
- **Mileage claims** require the date, route, and total miles; no fuel receipt is needed.
- Receipts must be uploaded to SpendDesk as a scan or photo at the time of claim.

## 7. Submission Deadlines

- Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the date the expense was incurred.
- Expenses submitted **31–60 days** after being incurred require a written reason and **line-manager approval** to be considered.
- Expenses submitted **more than 60 days** after being incurred are **not reimbursed**, except in documented exceptional circumstances approved by the **Finance Team**.
- All expenses for a given leave year must be submitted by **15 January** of the following year.

## 8. Approval Process

1. The employee enters the expense in **SpendDesk**, selects the category, and attaches receipts.
2. SpendDesk validates the claim against the limits in Section 5 and flags any item that exceeds a limit or is missing a required receipt.
3. The claim routes to the employee's **line manager**, who verifies the business purpose and that limits were respected.
4. The line manager **approves or rejects within 5 working days**. Rejections must include a reason.
5. Claims where the **line manager is the beneficiary**, or any claim **over $5,000**, route to the **Finance Team** for a second approval.
6. Approved claims are paid in the **next scheduled payment run**, normally within **10 working days** of final approval. Payment runs occur **weekly, on Fridays**.
7. Rejected claims can be corrected and resubmitted once, within the deadlines in Section 7.
