## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how employees accrue, request, and use paid leave. It applies to all permanent employees. Contractors and agency staff are covered by their individual agreements and are out of scope.

All leave is requested and approved through the HR system, **PeopleHub**.

## 2. Definitions

- **Full-time employee:** works the standard 37.5-hour week (09:00–17:30, Monday–Friday, with a 60-minute unpaid lunch).
- **Part-time employee:** works fewer than 37.5 hours per week; entitlements are pro-rated by contracted hours.
- **Probation period:** the first **6 months** of employment.
- **Leave year:** 1 January to 31 December.
- **Line manager:** the employee's direct manager of record in PeopleHub.

## 3. Annual Leave Entitlement

- Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid annual leave per leave year, in addition to **public holidays**.
- Annual leave **accrues monthly** at **2.08 days per completed month** of service.
- Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement (25 days × contracted hours ÷ 37.5).
- Employees may take accrued leave **during probation**, subject to the same approval process in Section 6.
- After **5 years** of continuous service, entitlement increases to **28 days** per leave year, effective the leave year following the fifth anniversary.

## 4. Sick Leave

- Employees receive up to **10 paid sick days** per leave year. These do not accrue and do not carry forward.
- **Days 1–2 (self-certification):** the employee must notify their line manager by **10:00** on the first day of absence, by phone or email, and log the absence in PeopleHub within 2 working days.
- **Day 3 and beyond (consecutive):** a **medical certificate** (doctor's note) must be uploaded to PeopleHub.
- Sickness beyond 10 paid days in a leave year moves to **statutory/unpaid sick leave**; the People Team will contact the employee to discuss support options.
- Sickness that occurs **during approved annual leave** may be reclassified as sick leave if supported by a medical certificate, restoring the affected annual leave days.

## 5. Other Leave Types

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Public holidays | Per national calendar | Paid; not deducted from annual leave |
| Bereavement leave | Up to 5 paid days per event (immediate family) | Approved by line manager; no deduction from annual leave |
| Parental leave | Per statutory entitlement | Coordinated with the People Team at least 15 weeks before the expected date |
| Unpaid leave | Up to 20 days per leave year | Requires line manager **and** People Team approval |

## 6. Leave Application Process

1. The employee submits a leave request in **PeopleHub**, selecting the leave type and dates.
2. Annual leave must be requested **at least 10 working days** before the start date for requests of 5 days or more, and **at least 3 working days** before for shorter requests.
3. The request is routed automatically to the employee's **line manager**.
4. The line manager must **approve or decline within 3 working days**. If no decision is recorded within 3 working days, the request is escalated automatically to the manager's manager.
5. The employee receives an email confirmation from PeopleHub when the request is approved or declined. Leave is only valid once it shows **"Approved"** in PeopleHub.

## 7. Approval Requirements

- **Line manager** approval is required for all annual, bereavement, and sick leave entries.
- **Line manager and People Team** approval is required for **unpaid leave** and for annual leave requests **longer than 15 consecutive days**.
- A request may only be declined for a documented business reason (for example, critical project deadlines or insufficient team coverage). The reason must be recorded in PeopleHub.
- During peak business periods (defined annually by each department head and published in PeopleHub), a maximum of **one third of a team** may be on annual leave at the same time.

## 8. Carry-Forward Rules

- Employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** into the next leave year.
- Carried-forward days must be used by **31 March** of the new leave year, after which they are forfeited.
- Days carried forward are used **before** the new year's accrued entitlement.
- Carry-forward above 5 days is only permitted where the employee was **prevented from taking leave for a documented business reason** and is approved by the **People Team** on a case-by-case basis.
- Unused sick leave and unpaid leave **cannot** be carried forward.

## 9. Leave on Termination

- On leaving the company, accrued-but-unused annual leave is **paid out** in the final salary.
- Annual leave taken **in excess** of the accrued entitlement is **deducted** from the final salary.
